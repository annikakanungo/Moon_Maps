Challenge context

Mission planning at the Moon’s south pole is constrained by extreme lighting and communication conditions. Small changes in landing site or date can affect power generation greatly thermal control, and direct-to-Earth contact. This tool quickly translates that complexity into an accessible, comparable view for mission planners, classroom use, and public engagement.

Objective

Enable rapid evaluation of landing site options through clear comparison of sunlight, horizon visibility, and operational windows.

Focus

South-pole terrain, crater shadowing, Earth visibility, and limits on solar power during lunar day and night transitions.

Audience

Mission planners, engineers, educators, and members of the public who need a simple way to compare lunar conditions.
